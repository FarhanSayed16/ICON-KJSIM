### Day 1 observations / risks

- Hackathon is **long** (8 AM–8 PM with eval). Needs food, power, Wi-Fi, mentors, and night exit plan.
- Tech Fair + Gallery + Football overlap morning — **volunteer shortage** is the #1 risk.
- Inauguration is short (30 min) — keep speeches tight; don’t delay Tech Fair open.
- Valedictory at 5:00 PM on Day 1 is **early** vs hackathon ending 8 PM — winners announced are only Football + Gallery (not hackathon). That’s OK if Day 2 has a bigger closing, or hackathon winners announced after eval / separately.

## What worked in this structure (keep the logic)

1. **One flagship brain event** all Day 1 (Hackathon).  
2. **One high-footfall public zone** (Tech Fair + Gallery).  
3. **Sports on turf**, not inside academic blocks.  
4. **Mind/gaming on Day 2 labs** when classrooms free.  
5. **Treasure Hunt morning Day 2** — campus energy without clashing hackathon.
